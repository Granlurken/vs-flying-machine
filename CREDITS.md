# Credits

## Sounds

The engine sounds (`FlyingMachine/assets/flyingmachine/sounds/engine/`) are trimmed and
looped from recordings on [Freesound](https://freesound.org), released under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). No attribution is
required, but thanks to the recordists all the same.

The brake sound is Vintage Story's own `game:sounds/effect/woodgrind.ogg` and is not
included in this repository.
